## FAQ

### Items
- **Q: What if I can't attend live?** Threshold is recorded and you'll have lifetime access. That said, the live session is meaningfully different — most of the value is in real-time presence with the group.
- **Q: I don't have a meditation practice. Will I keep up?** Yes. Threshold assumes no prior practice. The methods are taught from zero.
- **Q: How is this different from therapy?** Therapy investigates patterns over months or years. Threshold is a single four-hour intervention focused on one specific question or transition you bring in. It complements therapy; it does not replace it.
